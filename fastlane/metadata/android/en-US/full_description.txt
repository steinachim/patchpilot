Patch Pilot lets you browse, organize and edit the presets stored on a hardware synthesizer, directly from your phone or tablet over USB - rename, copy, move and delete presets without a computer or the manufacturer's own editor software.

Supported instruments

- Nord Grand, Nord Grand 2, Nord Stage 2 EX, Nord Stage 4, Nord Electro 7, Nord Piano 6, Nord Lead A1, Nord Wave 2
- Behringer Pro-800
- Yamaha Motif XS6 (the XS7 and XS8 are recognized as well, but not yet confirmed on real hardware)

Each instrument connects over USB, directly or via USB-MIDI where the instrument supports it. Other Nord keyboards that speak the same protocol can be used with a warning; the app's debug menu can run a regression test and share a report that helps get them officially supported.

No account, no ads, no internet access. The app has no network permission at all and never sends data anywhere - the only thing it talks to is the instrument plugged into your phone.

Not sure it fits your setup? Open the app without an instrument attached and choose "Try demo mode" to explore the preset browser on a simulated instrument.
